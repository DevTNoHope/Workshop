# Event Report: FCAJ Community Day (05/23/2026)

### 1. General Event Information
* **Event Name**: FCAJ Community Day (AWS First Cloud AI Journey)
* **Date & Time**: Saturday, May 23, 2026 (09:00 AM – 12:00 PM)
* **Location**: AWS Office, 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City
* **Role**: Attendee

---

### 2. Key Technology Topics Shared

#### Topic 1: Context-Driven Generative AI Applications
* **Speaker**: Tinh Truong
* The speaker explored why AI models often deliver generic or incorrect answers when they lack localized background information.
* He presented architectural blueprints for building a "Second Brain AI" that retains interaction history and contextual parameters to output personalized, highly accurate results.

#### Topic 2: Streamlining Operations with Amazon Q
* **Speaker**: Anh Pham
* Discussed how Amazon Q acts as an enterprise-wide assistant through four core components:
  * **Quick Chat Agent**: Enables interactive data querying.
  * **Quick Flows**: Powers no-code automation of routine tasks using conversational prompts.
  * **Quick Spaces**: Provides shared workspaces to compile and synthesize team knowledge.
  * **Quick Vision**: Generates reporting charts from raw metrics using natural language.

#### Topic 3: Securing and Optimizing Deployments via Amazon CloudFront
* **Speaker**: Thinh Nguyen
* Re-evaluated Amazon CloudFront's role, demonstrating that it is far more than a basic CDN.
* Key benefits discussed include edge protection against DDoS/WAF threats, reducing outbound data transfer expenses, and speeding up dynamic page responses for global users.

#### Topic 4: Practical Lessons from a 36-Hour Hackathon (LotusHacks)
* **Speaker**: Team VIB
* Shared the development story of UTMorpho under tight deadlines.
* Emphasized key team strategies: prioritizing Minimum Viable Products (MVPs), focusing on core value propositions, and remaining agile enough to pivot when technical bottlenecks occur.

#### Topic 5: Tackling Non-Determinism in Large Language Models
* **Speaker**: Duc Dao
* A technical analysis of LLM inference behaviors.
* The session showed that even with `temperature=0`, underlying hardware optimizations can lead to slight variations in token selection. The speaker shared practical coding practices to achieve more deterministic outputs in production environments.

#### Topic 6: Enterprise Multi-Agent Systems
* **Speaker**: Vy Lam
* Presented a case study of startup credit evaluation.
* The session proposed replacing single-agent architectures with a coordinated "Virtual Credit Committee" comprised of specialized sub-agents with built-in guardrails and compliance checks.

---

### 3. Key Takeaways & Career Development

* **Technical Insights**:
  * Gained a deeper understanding of LLM nondeterminism. This is highly useful for defining correct temperature limits and retry logics in my own application.
  * Realized how Amazon CloudFront can serve as a shield at the edge, saving bandwidth and caching API/S3 contents efficiently.
  * Identified the industry shift from simple chat agents to complex Multi-Agent committees for enterprise automation.

* **Practical Application**:
  * Networking at the AWS office helped me connect with active cloud and AI engineers in Vietnam.
  * I will apply these context-management and LLM reliability lessons directly to my **ITCoach** mock interview room project to evaluate essay and audio submissions more consistently.
